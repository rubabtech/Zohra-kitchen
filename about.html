Meet Esha Ahsan

Zohra Kitchen was founded by Esha Ahsan, a home baker who enjoys creating cakes for special occasions. Each custom order begins with a conversation about your ideas.

Whether you are planning a birthday, an engagement or a family celebration, explore our designs and tell us what you have in mind.

How to order

  1. Browse the cake designs.
  2. Fill in your booking preferences.
  3. Discuss availability and the final price on WhatsApp.
  4. Confirm delivery or collection with the baker.

What you can choose

  • Birthday and celebration cakes
  • Engagement and occasion cakes
  • Custom cupcakes
  • Brownies and muffins
